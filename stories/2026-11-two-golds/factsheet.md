## Core facts (used in script)
| Fact | Sources |
|---|---|
| Barshim (Qatar) and Tamberi (Italy) both cleared 2.37 m with no misses up to that point | CBC, WA-report, ESPN |
| Both failed three times at 2.39 m (an Olympic-record height) | CBC, ESPN, WA-report |
| An official offered a jump-off | CBC, ESPN, WA-feature |
| Barshim asked "Can we have two golds?" | CBC, Sky, ESPN, WA-feature |
| The official nodded / said it was possible | CBC, Sky (nodded); theScore (said possible) |
| Tamberi slapped Barshim's hand and leapt into his arms; later flopped onto the track, rolled, screamed | ESPN, CBC |
| Barshim called Tamberi one of his best friends | CBC, Sky, ESPN |
| Before Rio 2016 Tamberi suffered an ankle injury and was told he might not compete again | CBC, WA-report, Sky |
| Tamberi kept his cast for five years | ESPN, theScore |
| The cast was at the stadium (knelt beside it [WA-report]; used it to mark start of run-up [WA-feature]; picked up "the shell of an old cast" [NBC]) | WA-report, WA-feature, NBC |
| Barshim later broke down in tears | NBC |

## Other facts (not used)
- Maksim Nedasekau (Belarus) took bronze on countback, also 2.37 m — CBC, WA-report.
- Barshim cleared six heights, Tamberi seven; first two men ever to clear 2.37 m at an Olympics — WA-report.
- Barshim: London 2012 medal, Rio 2016 silver, world titles 2017 & 2019 — CBC.
- Tamberi was sidelined 11 months after the injury (Monaco Diamond League, attempting 2.41 m) — WA-report.
- Barshim knocked on Tamberi's hotel door after a 2017 no-height in Paris and insisted on talking — WA-feature.

## FLAGS — disagreements / unverified (kept OUT of script)
1. **Official's exact words** — no source quotes the official verbatim. CBC/Sky: he "nodded"; theScore: "said that was possible"; NBC: "allowed Barshim's request". Script uses narrator "The official nodded." No OFFICIAL voice line.
2. **Who Barshim asked** — CBC/Sky/WA-feature: asked the official. ESPN text says asked "of Tamberi". Script just has Barshim ask in front of both; does not specify.
3. **Cast inscription** — ESPN & theScore (AP): "Road to Tokyo 2020", later "2020" crossed out and "2021" written in red. NBC: "Road to Tokyo" with 2020 struck through and 2021 beside it. WA-report: "My road to Tokyo". Wording disputed -> cast shown PLAIN, no text.
4. **Nature/timing of Tamberi's injury** — CBC/Sky/ESPN: "broke his ankle", "days before" Rio. WA-report: tore a ligament in his left ankle, three weeks before Rio. Script says only "ankle cast" and "told he might never compete again" (his own quote).
5. **Whether Tamberi brought the cast** — ESPN/theScore silent; WA-report, WA-feature, NBC all place it at the stadium. Treated as verified (three sources), shown on track.
6. **Barshim's own injury** — WA-report: tore ankle ligaments July 2018 (Szekesfehervar, 2.46 m WR attempt), out 11 months; WA-feature: same ankle injury, needed surgery; Sky: "recovered from a serious injury". CBC/ESPN don't mention it. Not disputed so much as under-reported; left out of script for time.
7. **Barshim's tears / "laughs" tag** — "This is beyond sport" was said in post-event remarks, not necessarily on the track; dramatised placement only, words verbatim. "I dreamed of this so many times" likewise from post-event interview.
8. **Vest colours** in visuals are plain national colours only; no flags/text.
