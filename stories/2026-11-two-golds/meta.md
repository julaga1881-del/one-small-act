TITLE: They tied for Olympic gold… then he asked one question 🥇

DESCRIPTION:
Based on a true story, dramatised.

At the Tokyo 2020 Olympics, high jumpers Mutaz Essa Barshim of Qatar and Gianmarco Tamberi of Italy, close friends, both cleared 2.37 m without a miss, then both failed three times at 2.39 m. Offered a jump-off, Barshim asked a simple question instead, and the two shared the gold. For Tamberi, who kept his ankle cast for five years after an injury before Rio threatened his career, it was a dream come true.
