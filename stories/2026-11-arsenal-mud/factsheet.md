- Responders: Chulmleigh fire brigade first, then specialist teams, South Molton police, a boat rescue service, four ambulance crews, multiple helicopters. [SWNS][GNN]
- Cass made multiple trips up and down guiding crews and carrying equipment. [SWNS][GNN][K103]
- Rescue took two hours; Sallie freed. [SWNS][GNN]
- In hospital until 4 am next day; "well now". [SWNS]
- Thank-you card, plus a second card. [SWNS][GNN][K103]
